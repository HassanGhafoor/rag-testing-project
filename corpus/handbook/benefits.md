# Employee Benefits

**Effective date:** 1 January 2025
**Owner:** People Operations, Nimbus Logistics (Pvt) Ltd, Lahore

## Purpose

This document summarises the benefits available to permanent employees of
Nimbus Logistics. All monetary values are in Pakistani Rupees (PKR) unless
stated otherwise. Benefits begin after the 3-month probation period unless noted.

## Overview Table

| Benefit | Coverage / Amount | Eligibility |
|---------|-------------------|-------------|
| Health insurance (self) | Up to PKR 800,000 per year | From day one |
| Health insurance (family) | Up to PKR 500,000 per year | After probation |
| Provident fund | 8.33% of basic, matched by company | After probation |
| Gratuity | One month's basic per year of service | After 1 year |
| Annual medical checkup | Up to PKR 12,000 per year | After probation |
| Professional training budget | PKR 60,000 per year | After probation |
| Gym / wellness reimbursement | PKR 4,000 per month | After probation |
| Eid bonus | One month's basic salary | After 6 months |

## Health Insurance

Nimbus provides group health insurance through a panel of hospitals across
Lahore, Karachi, and Islamabad. Self-coverage begins on the first day of
employment; family coverage (spouse and up to 3 dependent children) begins after
probation. Claims are processed through the insurer's app within **10 working
days**.

## Provident Fund and Gratuity

Employees contribute **8.33%** of basic salary to the provident fund each month,
matched equally by the company. The fund vests fully after **2 years** of
continuous service. Gratuity is paid on separation at **one month's basic salary
per completed year**, for those with at least one year of service.

## Learning and Development

Each employee has an annual professional training budget of **PKR 60,000** for
courses, certifications, and conferences relevant to their role. Requests are
approved by the line manager and tracked in NimbusHR. Unused budget does not
carry forward.

## Wellness

A monthly wellness reimbursement of **PKR 4,000** covers gym membership, sports
facilities, or approved fitness programmes, claimed through NimbusClaim with
proof of payment.
