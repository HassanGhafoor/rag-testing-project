# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

**Effective date:** 1 February 2025
**Owner:** People Operations, Nimbus Logistics (Pvt) Ltd, Lahore

## Purpose

This policy explains how Nimbus Logistics supports remote and hybrid working for
roles where physical presence is not required every day. Fleet Operations,
Warehouse, and front-desk Admin roles are on-site by nature and are not eligible.

## Hybrid Schedule

Eligible employees work a **hybrid schedule of 3 days on-site and 2 days
remote** each week. On-site days are coordinated within each team so that the
whole team overlaps in the Lahore office on at least **2 common days** per week,
usually Tuesday and Thursday.

## Fully Remote Arrangements

Fully remote arrangements are approved case by case by the department head and
People Operations, and are reviewed every **6 months**. Fully remote employees
must attend the Lahore office for a minimum of **2 days per quarter** for team
planning, at company expense if travelling from another city.

## Core Hours

Regardless of location, all staff must be available during **core hours of
11:00 to 16:00 (PKT)**. Outside core hours, employees may flex their start and
end times as long as they complete their contracted hours and meet deadlines.

## Equipment and Allowance

Nimbus provides a company laptop for all remote-eligible staff (see
**laptop-policy.md**). Remote workers also receive a one-time home-office setup
allowance of **PKR 20,000**, claimable through NimbusClaim within the first
month of approval, and a monthly internet top-up of **PKR 2,500**.

## Attendance and Availability

Remote days must be logged in **NimbusHR** by 10:00 each morning. Employees are
expected to keep their calendar current and to respond to messages within
**2 hours** during core hours. Persistent unavailability may lead to the hybrid
arrangement being withdrawn.

## Data Security

When working remotely, employees must connect through the company VPN for access
to internal systems (see **vpn-setup.md**), lock their screens when away, and
never use public Wi-Fi without the VPN active. Company data must not be stored on
personal devices.

## Leave and Remote Work

Remote and hybrid schedules do not change leave entitlements. A remote day is a
working day, not a day off; leave for a remote day must still be requested
through NimbusHR (see **leave-policy.md**).

## Review

This policy is reviewed annually. Any change to an individual's arrangement is
confirmed in writing through People Operations.
