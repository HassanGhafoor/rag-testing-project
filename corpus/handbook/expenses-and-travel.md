# Expenses and Travel Policy

**Effective date:** 1 March 2025
**Owner:** Finance Department, Nimbus Logistics (Pvt) Ltd, Lahore
**Applies to:** All employees incurring business expenses

## Purpose

This policy sets out what business expenses Nimbus Logistics reimburses, the
limits that apply, and how to claim through our expense system, **NimbusClaim**.
All amounts are in Pakistani Rupees (PKR).

## General Principles

Expenses must be **reasonable, necessary, and business-related**. Employees are
expected to spend company money as carefully as they would their own. Every
claim above **PKR 1,000** requires a valid receipt or tax invoice uploaded to
NimbusClaim. Claims without supporting documentation are rejected automatically.

Claims must be submitted within **30 days** of the expense being incurred.
Claims older than 60 days require written approval from the Head of Finance and
may be declined.

## Domestic Travel

For business travel within Pakistan, the following daily limits apply:

- **Accommodation:** up to **PKR 12,000 per night** in Lahore, Karachi, and
  Islamabad; up to **PKR 8,000 per night** elsewhere.
- **Meals (per diem):** **PKR 3,500 per day**, not requiring individual receipts
  but capped as a flat allowance.
- **Local transport:** actual cost of ride-hailing or taxis, supported by
  receipts. Personal car use is reimbursed at **PKR 45 per kilometre**.

Intercity travel should be booked through the Admin team at least **5 working
days** in advance to secure corporate rates. Economy-class air travel is the
default for domestic flights over 6 hours of road travel.

## International Travel

International trips require **prior written approval** from the department head
and the Head of Finance before any booking. A trip budget must be entered in
NimbusClaim and approved in advance.

- **Accommodation:** up to **USD 120 per night** equivalent, reimbursed in PKR
  at the exchange rate on the transaction date.
- **Meals (per diem):** **USD 55 per day** equivalent.
- **Air travel:** economy class for flights under 8 hours; premium economy is
  permitted for flights over 8 hours with department-head approval.

A foreign-travel advance of up to **PKR 150,000** may be requested through
NimbusClaim and must be settled with receipts within 14 days of returning.

## Client Entertainment

Client meals and entertainment are reimbursable up to **PKR 6,000 per head**,
with the names of attendees and the client organisation recorded on the claim.
Alcohol is not reimbursable under any circumstances. Entertainment above
**PKR 25,000** in total requires pre-approval from the department head.

## Mobile and Internet

Employees in eligible roles receive a monthly communications allowance of
**PKR 3,000**, paid with salary and not requiring individual claims. Field and
Fleet Operations staff receive **PKR 4,500** to cover higher data usage.

## Non-Reimbursable Items

The following are never reimbursed:

- Personal purchases, fines, and traffic penalties
- Alcohol and tobacco
- Upgrades or add-ons not approved in advance
- Losses from failing to cancel a refundable booking on time
- Family or personal companion travel costs

## How to Claim

1. Open **NimbusClaim** and select **New Expense Claim**.
2. Enter the category, amount, date, and business reason.
3. Attach receipts for any line above **PKR 1,000**.
4. Submit. Claims route to your line manager, then to Finance.

Line managers must approve or return claims within **5 working days**. Approved
claims are paid in the next scheduled reimbursement run, processed on the
**10th and 25th** of each month.

## Advances

A general expense advance of up to **PKR 50,000** may be requested for planned
business activity. Advances not settled within 30 days are recovered from the
next salary payment.

## Questions

Direct expense queries to finance@nimbuslogistics.example. For travel booking
support, contact the Admin team through NimbusClaim's **Travel Desk** tab.
