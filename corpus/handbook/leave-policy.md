# Leave Policy

**Effective date:** 1 January 2025
**Owner:** People Operations, Nimbus Logistics (Pvt) Ltd, Lahore
**Applies to:** All permanent employees who have cleared probation

## Purpose

This policy defines the paid and unpaid leave that Nimbus Logistics grants to
permanent staff, how leave is accrued, and how it is requested through our
internal HR portal, **NimbusHR**. It replaces all leave guidance issued before
1 January 2025, including the 2023 handbook edition.

## Annual Leave

Permanent employees accrue **22 working days** of annual leave per calendar
year, credited at **1.83 days per completed month** of service. New joiners
begin accruing on their first day but may only take annual leave after clearing
the 3-month probation period.

A maximum of **10 unused annual leave days** may be carried forward into the
next calendar year. Carried-forward days must be used by **31 March**, after
which they lapse automatically in NimbusHR. Nimbus does not pay cash in lieu of
unused annual leave except on final settlement, where up to 10 days are encashed
at basic salary.

## Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to **12 days of paid sick leave** per calendar year. Sick
leave does not carry forward. For any absence of **3 consecutive days or more**,
a medical certificate from a registered practitioner must be uploaded to
NimbusHR within 48 hours of returning to work. Unused sick leave is never
encashed.

## Casual Leave

Nimbus grants **8 days of casual leave** per year for personal matters that
cannot be scheduled in advance. Casual leave may be taken in half-day units and
requires approval from the line manager at least **24 hours in advance**, except
in genuine emergencies.

## Maternity and Paternity Leave

Female employees are entitled to **90 calendar days** of fully paid maternity
leave, which may begin up to 4 weeks before the expected delivery date.
Male employees receive **10 working days** of paid paternity leave, to be taken
within 60 days of the child's birth. Both are granted independently of annual,
sick, and casual leave balances.

## Bereavement Leave

Up to **5 working days** of paid bereavement leave are granted on the death of
an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, or sibling), and **2 days**
for extended family. No documentation is required, though managers may request
confirmation for absences beyond the standard allowance.

## Public Holidays

Nimbus Logistics observes **14 public holidays** each year, published by People
Operations every December. Employees in operational roles who are rostered to
work on a public holiday receive a compensatory day off within 30 days, or a
holiday allowance of **PKR 4,500 per day** if a compensatory day cannot be
scheduled.

## Unpaid Leave

Where paid balances are exhausted, employees may request unpaid leave of up to
**30 calendar days** per year with the approval of both the line manager and the
Head of People Operations. Unpaid leave beyond 30 days requires written approval
from the CEO's office and may affect benefit continuity.

## How to Request Leave

1. Log in to **NimbusHR** and open the **Leave** tab.
2. Select the leave type, dates, and (if required) attach documentation.
3. Submit for approval. Requests route automatically to your line manager.
4. Track the status in **NimbusHR**; you will receive an email once approved or
   declined.

Annual leave requests should be submitted **at least 7 working days** before the
intended start date. Requests submitted with shorter notice may be declined for
operational reasons, particularly in the Fleet Operations and Warehouse teams
during peak season (November and December).

## Approval and Disputes

Line managers must action leave requests within **3 working days**. If a request
is not actioned within that window, it escalates automatically to the department
head via NimbusHR. Disputes over leave balances should be raised with People
Operations at people@nimbuslogistics.example.
