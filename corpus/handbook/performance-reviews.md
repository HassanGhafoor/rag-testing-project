# Performance Review Policy

**Effective date:** 1 January 2025
**Owner:** People Operations, Nimbus Logistics (Pvt) Ltd, Lahore

## Purpose

This policy describes how Nimbus Logistics evaluates employee performance, sets
goals, and links performance to development and rewards.

## Review Cycle

Nimbus runs **two formal reviews per year**: a mid-year check-in in **July** and
an annual review in **January**. Both are conducted in **NimbusHR** using the
standard review form. New joiners have their first review at the **end of
probation (month 3)**.

## Rating Scale

Performance is rated on a **5-point scale**:

1. Below expectations
2. Partially meets expectations
3. Meets expectations
4. Exceeds expectations
5. Outstanding

A rating of **3 or above** is considered a satisfactory annual outcome. A rating
of **1** triggers a formal Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).

## Goal Setting

At the start of each year, employees and managers agree on **4 to 6 objectives**,
each with measurable success criteria. Objectives are recorded in NimbusHR and
reviewed at the mid-year check-in. Objectives may be adjusted mid-year with
manager approval if business priorities change.

## Performance Improvement Plans

A PIP runs for **60 days**, with weekly check-ins between the employee and
manager. People Operations monitors every active PIP. Successful completion
returns the employee to normal review status; an unsuccessful PIP may lead to
role change or exit under the separation process.

## Promotions and Increments

Annual increments and promotions are decided after the January review, based on
the annual rating, sustained performance, and business budget. Increments take
effect from **1 April**. Promotion requires a documented case from the line
manager and endorsement from the department head.

## Feedback and Appeals

Employees may add written comments to their review in NimbusHR. If an employee
disagrees with a rating, they may appeal to the department head within
**10 working days** of the review being finalised. People Operations facilitates
appeals and records the outcome.

## Confidentiality

Review records are confidential and visible only to the employee, their line
manager, the department head, and People Operations.
