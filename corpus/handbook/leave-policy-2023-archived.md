> It is retained for historical reference only. Numbers below are outdated.

**Owner:** HR Department, Nimbus Logistics (Pvt) Ltd, Lahore

## Annual Leave (2023)

Permanent employees accrued **18 working days** of annual leave per calendar
year, credited at **1.5 days per completed month**. A maximum of **6 unused
days** could be carried forward, and carried-forward days had to be used by
**28 February** of the following year.

## Sick Leave (2023)

Employees received **10 days of paid sick leave** per year. A medical
certificate was required for absences of **4 consecutive days or more**.

## Casual Leave (2023)

**6 days of casual leave** were granted per year, requiring **48 hours** advance
notice to the line manager.

## Maternity and Paternity Leave (2023)

Maternity leave was **84 calendar days** fully paid. Paternity leave was
**7 working days**, to be taken within 45 days of the child's birth.

## Bereavement Leave (2023)

Up to **3 working days** for an immediate family member; **1 day** for extended
family.

## Public Holidays (2023)

Nimbus observed **12 public holidays** in 2023. The holiday working allowance was
**PKR 3,000 per day**.

## Unpaid Leave (2023)

Unpaid leave was capped at **21 calendar days** per year with line manager and
HR approval.

## Requesting Leave (2023)

Leave was requested by email to the HR Department and copied to the line manager.
The NimbusHR portal was not yet in use during this period. Annual leave required
**10 working days** advance notice.
