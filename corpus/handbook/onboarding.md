# Onboarding Guide

**Effective date:** 1 January 2025
**Owner:** People Operations, Nimbus Logistics (Pvt) Ltd, Lahore
**Audience:** New joiners and their line managers

## Purpose

This guide walks new employees through their first 90 days at Nimbus Logistics,
from the first day in the Lahore office to the end of probation. It also lists
what line managers and People Operations are responsible for at each stage.

## Before Day One

People Operations sends a welcome email at least **3 working days** before the
start date, containing the joining time, office address, dress code, and a
checklist of documents to bring: CNIC copy, academic certificates, two passport
photos, bank account details, and previous employer's experience letter.

The IT team pre-provisions a company laptop and creates accounts so that
credentials are ready on day one. The manager assigns an **onboarding buddy**
from the team.

## Day One

On the first day, new joiners:

1. Complete the joining formalities with People Operations and sign the
   employment contract.
2. Collect their company laptop and access card from IT and Admin.
3. Set up their **NimbusHR** and email accounts, and configure MFA (see
   **passwords-and-mfa.md**).
4. Install and test the VPN with the onboarding buddy (see **vpn-setup.md**).
5. Attend a 1-hour company orientation covering values, structure, and the
   code of conduct.

The onboarding buddy gives an office tour and introduces the team over lunch,
which is hosted by the company on the first day.

## First Week

During week one, the new joiner:

- Reviews the employee handbook, including **leave-policy.md**,
  **expenses-and-travel.md**, and **remote-work.md**.
- Meets each member of their immediate team in short one-on-ones.
- Completes mandatory **information security awareness training** in NimbusHR.
- Agrees an initial set of goals for the probation period with their manager.

The manager schedules a **30-minute check-in at the end of week one** to answer
questions and confirm the new joiner has working access to all required systems.

## First 30 Days

By the end of the first month, the new joiner should be contributing to
day-to-day work with support. The manager holds a **30-day review conversation**
covering early progress, any access or tooling gaps, and clarity of goals. Any
role-specific training (for example, warehouse safety or fleet dispatch systems)
is completed in this period.

## First 90 Days and Probation

Probation lasts **3 months**. At the end of probation, the manager completes a
formal probation review in NimbusHR, recommending confirmation, extension, or,
in rare cases, exit. Confirmation unlocks full benefits, including family health
coverage and provident fund enrolment (see **benefits.md**), and eligibility to
take annual leave.

## Roles and Responsibilities

- **New joiner:** complete formalities, training, and setup; raise blockers
  early.
- **Line manager:** set goals, run the week-one, 30-day, and probation reviews,
  and ensure the buddy is available.
- **Onboarding buddy:** be the day-to-day point of contact for informal
  questions in the first month.
- **People Operations:** manage documentation, orientation, and confirmation.
- **IT team:** provision hardware, accounts, and access before day one.

## Common First-Week Questions

- **How do I request leave?** Through NimbusHR — see **leave-policy.md**.
- **How do I claim expenses?** Through NimbusClaim — see
  **expenses-and-travel.md**.
- **How do I install software?** Raise a request — see **software-requests.md**.
- **My VPN won't connect.** See **it-faq.md** or contact the IT Helpdesk.

## Contact

For anything unclear during onboarding, contact your onboarding buddy first,
