# IT FAQ

**Owner:** IT & Cyber Security, Nimbus Logistics (Pvt) Ltd, Lahore

Quick answers to the questions the IT Helpdesk hears most often. For detail, see
the linked documents.

## Accounts and Passwords

**What are the password rules?**
Passwords must be at least **14 characters** long and must be rotated every
**90 days**. The last 5 passwords cannot be reused. See **passwords-and-mfa.md**.

**I forgot my password. What do I do?**
Use the **Reset Password** link on the NimbusHR login page. If you are locked
out, contact the IT Helpdesk.

**Where should I store my passwords?**
In **NimbusVault**, the approved password manager — never in a browser or
spreadsheet.

## MFA

**My MFA code isn't working.**
Check that the time on your authenticator app (NimbusAuth) is synced. If it still
fails, contact the IT Helpdesk for a temporary access code.

**I lost my phone with the authenticator.**
Contact the IT Helpdesk immediately. After identity verification, IT issues a
temporary access code valid for 30 minutes. See **passwords-and-mfa.md**.

## VPN

**How do I connect to the VPN?**
Install and open **NimbusConnect**, request your profile, then click Connect. See
**vpn-setup.md**.

**The VPN says my profile expired.**
Profiles expire after 90 days of inactivity. Click **Request Profile** in
NimbusConnect to get a new one.

## Laptops and Software

**How do I install software?**
Use the **Nimbus Software Portal** for approved apps, or submit a request for
anything else. See **software-requests.md**.

**My laptop was lost or stolen.**
Report it to the IT Helpdesk immediately so it can be locked and wiped. See
**laptop-policy.md**.

**How often are laptops replaced?**
The standard refresh cycle is every 4 years, or sooner if a device fails.

## Getting Help

**How do I reach IT?**
Raise a ticket in the **Nimbus Software Portal** or email
it.helpdesk@nimbuslogistics.example. Urgent issues affecting operations can be
called in to the Helpdesk line during business hours (09:00–18:00 PKT).
