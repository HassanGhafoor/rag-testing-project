# Company Laptop Policy

**Owner:** IT & Cyber Security, Nimbus Logistics (Pvt) Ltd, Lahore

## Purpose

This policy covers the issue, use, care, and return of company laptops at
Nimbus Logistics.

## Issue and Standard Image

Every eligible employee receives a company laptop provisioned with the standard
Nimbus image, which includes disk encryption, the endpoint security agent,
NimbusConnect (VPN), and the approved software set. Laptops are issued on or
before the first day (see **onboarding.md**). The standard refresh cycle is
**every 4 years**, or sooner if a device fails.

## Acceptable Use

Company laptops are provided for business use. Limited personal use is permitted
as long as it is lawful, does not affect performance, and does not break any
company policy. The following are **not permitted**:

- Installing unlicensed or unapproved software (see **software-requests.md**)
- Disabling disk encryption or the endpoint security agent
- Sharing the laptop with family members or third parties
- Storing company data on personal cloud accounts

## Security Requirements

- Keep the laptop locked when unattended, in the office and outside it.
- Apply operating-system and security updates within **7 days** of release; the
  endpoint agent enforces critical updates automatically.
- Connect through the VPN for internal systems when off the office network (see
  **vpn-setup.md**).
- Never leave the laptop visible in a vehicle or unattended in a public place.

## Damage, Loss, or Theft

Report a lost or stolen laptop to the **IT Helpdesk immediately** so the device
can be remotely locked and wiped and any VPN profile revoked. For theft, also
file a report with the local police and share the reference with IT. Accidental
damage should be reported within **24 hours**; IT assesses whether repair or
replacement is needed. Repeated negligent damage may be recovered from the
employee per the asset agreement.

## Returning the Laptop

On separation or role change, return the laptop, charger, and any accessories to
IT on or before the **last working day**. IT wipes the device and updates the
asset register. Salary final settlement may be held until company assets are
returned.

## Asset Register

All laptops are tracked in the IT asset register with a unique **Nimbus Asset
Tag**. Do not remove or deface the asset tag sticker.
