# Passwords and Multi-Factor Authentication

**Owner:** IT & Cyber Security, Nimbus Logistics (Pvt) Ltd, Lahore

## Purpose

This document explains the password and multi-factor authentication (MFA) rules
for all Nimbus Logistics accounts, including email, NimbusHR, NimbusClaim, and
the VPN.

## Password Rules

Passwords must be at least **14 characters** long and must be rotated every
**90 days**. The system prevents reuse of your last **5 passwords** and blocks
common or previously breached passwords.

A strong Nimbus password should:

- Combine upper- and lower-case letters, numbers, and symbols
- Avoid dictionary words, your name, or "Nimbus" in any form
- Not be reused on any personal account

You will receive a reminder in NimbusHR **7 days** before your password expires.
If a password expires, you must reset it before you can log in again.

## Using the Password Manager

Nimbus provides **NimbusVault**, the approved password manager, to every
employee. Store all work credentials in NimbusVault rather than in browsers,
notes, or spreadsheets. NimbusVault can generate compliant 14-character-plus
passwords automatically.

## Multi-Factor Authentication

MFA is **mandatory** on all Nimbus accounts. During onboarding you register an
authenticator app (the approved app is **NimbusAuth**, available in the Software
Portal). MFA is required at every login from a new device and once every **8
hours** on trusted devices.

### Setting Up MFA

1. Install **NimbusAuth** from the Nimbus Software Portal.
2. Log in to NimbusHR and open **Security → MFA Setup**.
3. Scan the QR code with NimbusAuth.
4. Enter the 6-digit code to confirm. Save the backup codes somewhere safe in
   NimbusVault.

## Lost Device or Locked Out

If you lose your MFA device or are locked out, contact the **IT Helpdesk**
immediately. IT verifies your identity before issuing a temporary access code
valid for **30 minutes**. Never share MFA codes with anyone, including IT staff —
IT will never ask you to read out a code.

## Reporting Suspicious Activity

Report suspected phishing, credential theft, or unexpected MFA prompts to
security@nimbuslogistics.example straight away. An unexpected MFA prompt may mean
someone has your password; change it immediately and alert IT.
